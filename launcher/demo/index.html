About this prototype

An unofficial Android launcher concept for Risk of Rain 2. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the game’s creators.

All files, storage, preparation and launches are simulated. Nothing is uploaded, converted, installed or played. A real launcher would require your own legally obtained game files; Android compatibility and phone-only preparation remain unverified.

Progress resets on reload. Diagnostic reports contain only this demo’s state, not personal information or device details.
